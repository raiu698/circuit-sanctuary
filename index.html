What I fix

Most of it is repairable

A lot of what gets thrown out only needs one part or one connection. Bring it by before you replace it — diagnosis costs you nothing but a phone call.

Game consoles

Overheating, HDMI ports, disc drives, drift on controllers, fan noise, no-power. Older systems too.

Sound systems

Receivers, amps, speakers, blown channels, crackling inputs, and gear that hums when it shouldn't.

Phones & tablets

Screens, batteries, charge ports, water damage. Plus rooting and customization if you want the thing to actually be yours.

Spam call blocking

I'll set your phone up so the robocalls stop getting through. Takes about twenty minutes and it sticks.

General electronics

TVs, monitors, small appliances, power supplies, odd one-off gear. If it plugs in, ask me.

Tweaks & setup

Speeding up a slow machine, cleaning up a phone, getting a system running the way you actually want it.
