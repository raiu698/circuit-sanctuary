Yes — overheating, HDMI ports, disc drives, fan noise, no-power faults, controller stick drift. Older consoles too.

Most jobs are a day or two, some same-day. If a part has to be ordered it depends on shipping, and I'll tell you that up front.

Do you pick up and deliver?

Yes. If you're close to Haleyville I can usually pick up and deliver it back to you. Further out, dropping it off is easier. Call and we'll work it out.

Can you stop the spam calls?
